Our History

Santorini was founded in the heart of Leiria with a clear vision: to transform the automotive service experience into something simpler, more transparent, and more reliable for everyone. From the very beginning, we believed that repairing a vehicle is only part of our work.

Our true commitment lies in providing peace of mind, safety, and confidence to every customer who entrusts us with their vehicle. With over 15 years of experience in the automotive sector, Santorini has built a solid reputation based on professionalism, technical precision, and absolute dedication to quality.

Over the years, we have become a benchmark in bodywork and paint, recognised for the excellence of our finishes and attention to every detail. Our company has been strategically structured to offer a complete, efficient, and accessible service, capable of meeting both the demands of private customers and the needs of corporate fleets.

We continuously invest in knowledge, processes, and service quality, because we believe every vehicle deserves the highest standard of care. But what truly defines Santorini goes beyond technique and experience. It is the relationship of trust we build with every customer. Here, each person is welcomed with respect, closeness, and commitment.

Every vehicle is treated with the care it deserves. Because we know that behind every car, there is a story, a routine, and a life that needs to go on. At Santorini, we do more than repair vehicles — we build trust every day.
